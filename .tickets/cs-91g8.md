# FollowingChanged message carries a redundant 'log' back-reference

**File**: claude_storm/widgets.py | **Line(s)**: 24-30 | **Description**: FollowingChanged stores self.log (the originating SelectableRichLog) but the app handler on_selectable_rich_log_following_changed never uses message.log — it independently calls query_one. The attribute is unused and adds unnecessary surface area. **Suggested Fix**: Remove the log parameter from FollowingChanged.__init__ and post self.FollowingChanged(following) instead of self.FollowingChanged(self, following).
